# Protective Bellows Fabric 
## Product Overview
 
Protective bellows are widely used on industrial machinery to shield moving parts from dust, metal chips, oil and sparks. To ensure reliable performance under continuous movement, the fabric used in bellows must provide both flexibility and durability.

PVC-coated fire-resistant fabric is commonly selected for bellows manufacturing due to its ability to withstand repeated folding while helping protect equipment in demanding industrial environments.

## Packaging

The fabric can be pre-folded to the required fold width before shipment, such as 25 cm, 30 cm or 35 cm, according to customer specifications.

The folded fabric is wrapped in kraft paper and packed in wooden cases for safe international transportation.
