# Industrial Chain Bushes 
## Product Overview

Industrial chain bushes are precision components used in double pitch roller chains and industrial transmission systems. They provide stable chain movement, wear resistance and reliable performance for industrial machinery applications.

## Technical Specifications

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Product Type | Chain Bush / Sleeve Bushing |
| Material | Q195 Steel |
| Manufacturing Process | Precision Machining, Carburizing & Heat Treatment |
| Surface Treatment | Oiled / Anti-rust Coating |
| Hardness | HRA76-79 (After Heat Treatment) |
| Tolerance | +/-0.01-0.05 mm |
| Applications | Industrial Chains, Conveyor Systems, Machinery Transmission |

## Technical Notes

- **Carburizing & Heat Treatment:** Improves surface hardness and wear resistance for longer service life.
- **Tolerance +/-0.01-0.05 mm:** Ensures accurate dimensions and reliable assembly with chain components.
- **Surface Protection:** Bushes are coated with oil before shipment to help prevent rust and corrosion during storage and international transportation.

## Packaging

Industrial chain bushes are packed according to export requirements to ensure safe transportation during international shipping.

Bulk quantities can be packed in cartons, wooden cases or customized packaging solutions according to customer requirements.
