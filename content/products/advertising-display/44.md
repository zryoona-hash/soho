# 3D Holographic Fan Display
## Product Overview

A 3D Holographic Fan is a rotating LED display device that uses high-speed spinning LED blades to create the illusion of floating 3D images and videos in mid-air. It combines LED technology with synchronized visual effects to deliver vivid, dynamic 3D presentations without a traditional display panel.

Supporting custom image and video content, it is available in various sizes, resolutions, and configurations.

Applications: Shopping malls, retail stores, trade shows, exhibitions, museums, showrooms, restaurants, hotels, entertainment venues, and advertising events.
